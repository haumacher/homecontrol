HomeControl

Install the HomeControl firmware on an ESP32-C6 board and connect it to your WiFi, all from this page.

This browser cannot talk to serial ports. Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop computer. Serial ports are only available on a secure page (HTTPS or localhost).
  1. Connect the board by USB and click Connect; choose the USB JTAG/serial debug unit.
  2. Install the firmware. Afterwards the page offers to set up WiFi: pick your network and enter its key. The status LED blinks fast while the device connects.
  3. Once connected, the device is reachable as homecontrol-xxxxxx.local (the last six hex digits of its MAC address).

The WiFi setup is offered for 5 minutes after the board starts, and for one minute after it is online. Reconnect (which restarts the board) to get it again. Without this page: press the router's WPS button, or use the serial console (wifi set).
